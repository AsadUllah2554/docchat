# Retainer and Support Terms

Fieldwork Studio Ltd — sample corpus for the DocChat demo. Fictional company; not legal advice.

Version 2.4, effective 1 February 2024. These terms apply to every Retainer and form part of the Master Services Agreement.

## 1. How Retainers work

A Retainer reserves a fixed number of Studio hours each month for ongoing design, development, maintenance and support. Hours can be used for any work within the Studio's services, including small features, content updates, performance work, bug fixes and advice. Larger pieces of work of more than 40 hours are normally scoped as a separate Project instead.

## 2. Plans and included hours

### 2.1 Plans

| Plan | Hours per month | Monthly fee | Response time for urgent issues |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essential | 10 | £900 | Next Business Day |
| Growth | 20 | £1,700 | 4 business hours |
| Priority | 40 | £3,200 | 1 business hour |

Fees exclude VAT and are invoiced monthly in advance, as set out in clause 3.2 of the Master Services Agreement.

### 2.2 Minimum term

Every Retainer has a minimum term of three months. After the minimum term it renews monthly and may be terminated with 30 days' notice under clause 4.2 of the Master Services Agreement. Notice given during the minimum term takes effect at the end of the minimum term or 30 days after notice, whichever is later.

### 2.3 Changing plan

The Client may move to a higher plan from the start of any month by giving notice before the 20th of the previous month. Moving to a lower plan is allowed once every three months, with the same notice.

## 3. Unused and additional hours

### 3.1 Rollover

Unused hours roll over to the following month only, up to a maximum of 50% of the plan's monthly hours. Rolled-over hours are used first and expire at the end of the month they were rolled into. Unused hours are not refunded and have no cash value, including on termination.

### 3.2 Overage

If the Client needs more hours than are available in a month, the Studio will ask for approval before exceeding the allowance. Approved additional hours are charged at the rates in clause 3.4 of the Master Services Agreement and invoiced at the end of the month.

### 3.3 Time tracking

Hours are tracked in 15-minute increments against named tasks. The Client can see a live time report at any time, and receives a monthly summary with the invoice.

## 4. Support and response times

### 4.1 Severity levels

- **Urgent**: the live website or application is down, a security incident is suspected, or payments cannot be taken.
- **High**: a key feature is broken for many users, with no workaround.
- **Normal**: a defect with a workaround, or a feature that is broken for a small number of users.
- **Request**: a change, improvement, content update or question.

### 4.2 Response and resolution targets

Response time is the time until a named person at the Studio acknowledges the issue and starts work. The urgent response time depends on the plan, as shown in clause 2.1. For all plans, High issues are responded to within one Business Day, Normal issues within two Business Days, and Requests are scheduled within five Business Days.

The Studio aims to resolve Urgent issues within one Business Day of response, but resolution times are targets, not guarantees, because they can depend on third parties such as hosting providers.

### 4.3 Support hours

Support hours are 9:00 to 17:30 UK time on Business Days. Out-of-hours emergency support is only available on the Priority plan, for Urgent issues, by phoning the emergency number in the onboarding pack. Out-of-hours work is charged at 1.5 times the normal rate and counts against the plan's hours at that multiple.

### 4.4 How to report an issue

Issues must be reported through the support portal or by emailing the support address. Issues reported by other channels, such as direct messages to individual staff, are not covered by the response targets.

## 5. Service credits

If the Studio misses an Urgent response target on the Growth or Priority plan, the Client may claim a service credit of 10% of that month's Retainer fee per missed target, up to a maximum of 30% of the monthly fee. Credits must be claimed within 30 days and are applied to the next invoice. Service credits are the Client's only financial remedy for missed response targets.

## 6. Pausing a Retainer

The Client may pause a Retainer once per calendar year for up to two consecutive months, by giving at least 14 days' written notice. No fees are charged during a pause, and rolled-over hours are kept until the end of the first month after the pause. Urgent support is not available while a Retainer is paused. A pause does not count towards the minimum term in clause 2.2.

## 7. Hosting and third-party services

### 7.1 Managed hosting

Where the Studio manages hosting for the Client, hosting is billed separately from the Retainer, and the Studio monitors uptime and applies security updates monthly. Time spent on routine hosting maintenance does not count against Retainer hours.

### 7.2 Client-managed services

Where the Client manages its own hosting, domains or third-party services, the Studio's response targets start only once it has the access it needs. Outages of third-party services are outside the Studio's control and are not covered by service credits.

## 8. What is not included

Retainer hours do not cover: work for a different website or application than the one named in the SOW, legal or accessibility audits by certified auditors, paid advertising spend, or content writing beyond light editing. These can be quoted separately.
