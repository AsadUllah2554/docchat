# Client Onboarding Guide

Fieldwork Studio Ltd — sample corpus for the DocChat demo. Fictional company; not legal advice.

This guide explains what happens between signing an SOW and the start of work, and what the Studio needs from new clients. It is practical guidance; the contract terms are in the Master Services Agreement.

## 1. After you sign

Within two Business Days of receiving a signed SOW, the Studio sends the first invoice (see clause 3.1 of the Master Services Agreement), a welcome email naming your project lead, and a link to book the kickoff meeting. Work is scheduled once the first invoice is paid.

## 2. Kickoff meeting

The kickoff is a 60 to 90 minute video call with your decision-maker and anyone who will give feedback. It covers goals, success measures, the delivery plan, how feedback and approvals will work, and the dates you will need to supply content. For Retainers, the kickoff also agrees the priority list for the first month.

## 3. Access we need

### 3.1 Systems

Please give the Studio access to the systems named in the SOW, such as your hosting account, domain registrar, analytics, content management system and any third-party services. Invite the named Studio people to your own accounts rather than sharing a login; see section 3 of the Information Security Policy.

### 3.2 Brand and content

Send your brand guidelines, logo files, fonts you hold licences for, and any existing content in editable formats. If you do not have brand guidelines, the Studio can create a short style guide as part of the design phase for an agreed fee.

## 4. Tools and communication

The Studio uses a shared project board for tasks and approvals, a shared channel for day-to-day questions, and email for formal decisions and notices. You will get an account on the project board at kickoff. Weekly status updates are sent every Friday, as described in section 5 of the Project Delivery Handbook.

## 5. Your team's time

Plan for your decision-maker to spend about two hours a week during discovery and design, and about one hour a week during build. Checkpoint approvals need a reply within five Business Days to keep to the timeline.

## 6. Retainer onboarding

Retainer clients receive an onboarding pack with the support portal link, the support email address, and, on the Priority plan, the out-of-hours emergency phone number. The first month of a Retainer includes up to three hours of setup time at no charge, for access, documentation review and monitoring setup; this does not reduce the plan's included hours.

## 7. Invoicing details

Send the Studio the name and email address of the person who should receive invoices, your purchase order number if your finance team needs one on every invoice, and your VAT number if you have one. Invoices are sent by email as PDF.
