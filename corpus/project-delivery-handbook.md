# Project Delivery Handbook

Fieldwork Studio Ltd — sample corpus for the DocChat demo. Fictional company; not legal advice.

Version 1.8. This handbook describes how the Studio runs Projects. It forms part of the Master Services Agreement, which takes precedence where they conflict (clause 1.2 of the Agreement).

## 1. Phases

Every Project runs through five phases: discovery, design, build, acceptance and launch. Each phase ends with a checkpoint where the Client approves the work before the next phase starts. Timelines in the SOW assume the Client gives feedback within five Business Days at each checkpoint.

### 1.1 Discovery

Discovery lasts one to three weeks. It produces a written brief, a sitemap or feature list, and a delivery plan. If the Client decides not to continue after discovery, only the discovery fee in the SOW is payable.

### 1.2 Design

Design includes up to two rounds of revisions on each agreed design deliverable. A round of revisions is one consolidated set of written feedback from the Client. Further rounds are charged as additional work under clause 3.4 of the Master Services Agreement.

### 1.3 Build

The Studio builds in two-week cycles and shares a working staging site at the end of each cycle. The Client can comment on the staging site at any time; comments are addressed in the next cycle unless they change the agreed scope, in which case section 4 applies.

## 2. Content

The Client supplies final copy, images and other content by the content deadline in the SOW, normally two weeks before the start of acceptance testing. If content arrives late, the Studio will load it at the next available opportunity, which may move the launch date. Content loading of more than 20 pages is quoted separately.

## 3. Acceptance testing

### 3.1 Test period

When the build is complete, the Studio notifies the Client that acceptance testing has started. The Client has 10 Business Days to test the Deliverables against the SOW and report defects in writing.

### 3.2 Deemed acceptance

If the Client does not report any defect within the test period, or starts using the Deliverables in live operation, the Deliverables are deemed accepted. Acceptance triggers the final Project invoice under clause 3.1 of the Master Services Agreement and starts the 30-day defect warranty in clause 7.1.

### 3.3 Defects versus changes

A defect is something that does not work as described in the SOW or in the approved designs. A request for something to work differently from the SOW is a change, handled under section 4.

## 4. Change requests

### 4.1 Raising a change

Either party may propose a change to the scope, timeline or Deliverables of a Project. Changes must be requested in writing, by email or through the project management tool.

### 4.2 Estimates

The Studio will reply with an estimate of the cost and timeline impact within three Business Days. Small changes estimated at under two hours may be absorbed at the Studio's discretion, up to a total of eight hours per Project.

### 4.3 Approval

No change work starts until the Client approves the estimate in writing. Approved changes are billed at the rates in clause 3.4 of the Master Services Agreement, either with the next milestone invoice or separately.

## 5. Communication

Each Project has a named project lead at the Studio and a single decision-maker at the Client. The Studio sends a written status update every Friday covering progress, risks and decisions needed. Standing check-in calls are weekly during build and fortnightly otherwise.

## 6. Launch

The Studio agrees a launch date and time with the Client at least five Business Days in advance. Launches are not scheduled on Fridays or the day before a public holiday. The Studio monitors the site for 48 hours after launch and fixes launch issues as a priority, under the defect warranty.

## 7. Handover

Within 10 Business Days of launch, the Studio provides handover documentation: admin guides, a list of third-party services and accounts, credentials transferred to the Client's password manager, and a list of significant open-source components with their licences (see clause 5.3 of the Master Services Agreement). One 90-minute training session for up to six people is included; further training is charged as additional work.

## 8. After launch

After the 30-day defect warranty ends, fixes and changes are handled under a Retainer or as additional work. Clients without a Retainer are responsible for security updates and hosting, unless they have a managed hosting arrangement under section 7 of the Retainer and Support Terms.
