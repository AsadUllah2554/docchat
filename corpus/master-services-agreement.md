# Master Services Agreement

Fieldwork Studio Ltd — sample corpus for the DocChat demo. Fictional company; not legal advice.

Version 3.1, effective 1 February 2024. This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") governs every engagement between Fieldwork Studio Ltd ("the Studio") and the client named in a Statement of Work ("the Client").

## 1. Definitions

### 1.1 Defined terms

"Statement of Work" or "SOW" means a document signed by both parties describing specific services, deliverables, timelines and fees, which incorporates this Agreement by reference.

"Retainer" means an ongoing monthly engagement for a fixed number of hours, on the terms set out in the Retainer and Support Terms.

"Project" means a fixed-scope engagement with defined deliverables and a fixed or estimated fee.

"Deliverables" means the work product the Studio is required to provide under an SOW, including designs, source code, documentation and content.

"Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in England.

### 1.2 Order of precedence

If there is a conflict between documents, the following order applies: first the SOW, then this Agreement, then the Retainer and Support Terms, then the Project Delivery Handbook. An SOW may only override this Agreement where it names the clause it overrides.

## 2. Services

### 2.1 Scope

The Studio will provide the services described in each SOW with reasonable skill and care, using suitably experienced staff. Work outside the scope of an SOW is only carried out under a written change request, as described in section 4 of the Project Delivery Handbook.

### 2.2 Client responsibilities

The Client will provide timely access to people, content, systems and decisions reasonably required for the Studio to perform the services. Where the Client does not provide content or feedback by the dates agreed in the SOW, the Studio may adjust the timeline, and any additional effort caused by the delay may be charged at the rates in clause 3.4.

### 2.3 Subcontractors

The Studio may use subcontractors for specialist work such as illustration, copywriting or penetration testing. The Studio remains responsible for work performed by its subcontractors and ensures they are bound by confidentiality obligations no less strict than clause 6.

## 3. Fees and payment

### 3.1 Project fees

Unless the SOW says otherwise, Projects are invoiced as follows: 40% on signature of the SOW, 40% on design approval, and 20% on launch or acceptance, whichever comes first. The first invoice must be paid before work begins.

### 3.2 Retainer fees

Retainers are invoiced monthly in advance on the first Business Day of each month. Hours included in a Retainer are described in section 2 of the Retainer and Support Terms.

### 3.3 Payment terms

Invoices are payable within 14 days of the invoice date by bank transfer. All fees are in pounds sterling (GBP) and exclude VAT, which is added at the prevailing rate.

### 3.4 Additional work and rates

Work outside an SOW, or beyond the hours included in a Retainer, is charged at £95 per hour for design and development and £120 per hour for technical architecture and security work, billed in 15-minute increments.

### 3.5 Late payment

If an invoice is unpaid 7 days after its due date, the Studio may charge interest at 1.5% per month on the overdue amount, and may suspend work after giving 5 Business Days' written notice. Suspension under this clause does not extend any deadline the Client is relying on.

### 3.6 Expenses

Third-party costs such as stock imagery, fonts, plugins and software licences are passed through at cost, and only with the Client's prior approval for any single item above £250.

## 4. Term and termination

### 4.1 Term

This Agreement starts on the date of the first SOW and continues until all SOWs have ended, unless terminated earlier under this section.

### 4.2 Termination for convenience

Either party may terminate a Retainer by giving thirty (30) days' written notice to the other party. Notice may be given by email to the contacts named in the SOW. Fees for the notice period remain payable, and hours in the notice period are available to the Client as normal.

A Project may be terminated by the Client at any time with written notice. On termination of a Project, the Client pays for all work performed up to the termination date at the rates in clause 3.4, capped at the total Project fee, and any non-cancellable third-party costs already committed.

### 4.3 Renewal of Retainers

Retainers renew automatically each month unless terminated under clause 4.2 or paused under section 6 of the Retainer and Support Terms. The Studio may change Retainer rates once per calendar year by giving at least 60 days' written notice; the Client may terminate under clause 4.2 if it does not accept the new rates.

### 4.4 Termination for cause

Either party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW immediately by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within 15 Business Days of receiving written notice describing the breach, or if the other party becomes insolvent.

### 4.5 Effect of termination

On termination, the Client pays all outstanding invoices and fees for work performed. The Studio delivers all completed and in-progress Deliverables that have been paid for, in their current state, within 10 Business Days. Clauses 5, 6, 8, 9 and 11 survive termination.

## 5. Intellectual property

### 5.1 Ownership of Deliverables

Ownership of the intellectual property rights in the Deliverables transfers to the Client on payment in full of all fees due under the relevant SOW. Until then, the Client has a licence to use the Deliverables for review and testing only.

### 5.2 Studio materials

The Studio retains ownership of its pre-existing tools, code libraries, templates and know-how ("Studio Materials"). Where Studio Materials are included in a Deliverable, the Client receives a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use them as part of that Deliverable.

### 5.3 Third-party and open-source components

Deliverables may include open-source software and third-party assets, which are licensed under their own terms. The Studio will list significant open-source components in the handover documentation described in section 7 of the Project Delivery Handbook.

### 5.4 Portfolio use

Unless the SOW says otherwise, the Studio may show the Deliverables in its portfolio and name the Client as a client, after the work is publicly launched. The Studio will not disclose confidential information in doing so.

## 6. Confidentiality

### 6.1 Obligations

Each party will keep the other party's confidential information secret, use it only for the purposes of this Agreement, and disclose it only to employees and subcontractors who need to know it. These obligations last for three years after termination, or indefinitely for trade secrets and personal data.

### 6.2 Exceptions

Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that is public through no fault of the receiving party, was already known to it, is independently developed, or must be disclosed by law.

## 7. Warranties

### 7.1 Defect warranty

For 30 days after launch or acceptance of a Project, the Studio will fix, free of charge, any defect that causes the Deliverables not to function as described in the SOW. This warranty does not cover issues caused by changes made by the Client or third parties, or by hosting environments not managed by the Studio.

### 7.2 Exclusions

Except as set out in clause 7.1, the Deliverables are provided without other warranties, including any warranty that they will be error-free or uninterrupted, to the extent permitted by law.

## 8. Liability

### 8.1 Cap

Each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the total fees paid by the Client in the six months before the event giving rise to the claim.

### 8.2 Excluded losses

Neither party is liable for loss of profits, revenue, data or goodwill, or for any indirect or consequential loss. Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or for any liability that cannot be limited by law.

## 9. Data protection

Where the Studio processes personal data on the Client's behalf, it acts as a processor under the UK GDPR and the Data Processing Addendum in Schedule 2 applies. How the Studio handles personal data as a controller, including its own client and contact records, is described in the Privacy Notice.

## 10. Non-solicitation

During an SOW and for 12 months after it ends, neither party will directly solicit for employment any employee of the other party who worked on the SOW, without the other party's written consent. General recruitment advertising is not solicitation.

## 11. Disputes and governing law

### 11.1 Escalation

The parties will first try to resolve any dispute through a meeting between their project leads within 10 Business Days, then between directors.

### 11.2 Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.

## 12. General

### 12.1 Notices

Formal notices under clauses 4.4 and 11 must be sent by email and by post to the registered address of the receiving party. Operational notices, including notice under clause 4.2, may be given by email alone.

### 12.2 Changes to this Agreement

The Studio may update this Agreement for future SOWs. Changes do not apply to an SOW already signed unless both parties agree in writing.

### 12.3 Force majeure

Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control. If such an event lasts more than 30 days, either party may terminate the affected SOW by written notice.
