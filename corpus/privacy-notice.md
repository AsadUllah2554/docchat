# Privacy Notice

Fieldwork Studio Ltd — sample corpus for the DocChat demo. Fictional company; not legal advice.

Last updated 15 January 2024. This notice explains how Fieldwork Studio Ltd ("we", "the Studio") collects and uses personal data as a controller. Where we process personal data on a client's behalf, we act as a processor and the Data Processing Addendum in Schedule 2 of the Master Services Agreement applies instead.

## 1. Who we are

Fieldwork Studio Ltd is a company registered in England and Wales. Our data protection lead can be contacted at the privacy address listed on our website. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office.

## 2. Data we collect

### 2.1 Client and contact data

Names, job titles, work email addresses and phone numbers of people at our clients and prospective clients, and records of our correspondence and meetings with them.

### 2.2 Billing data

Company names, billing addresses, VAT numbers, and records of invoices and payments. We do not store card numbers; card payments are handled by our payment provider.

### 2.3 Website visitors

If you visit our website, we collect pages viewed, approximate location derived from your IP address, browser type and referring site, using privacy-focused analytics that do not use advertising cookies. See section 8 for cookies.

### 2.4 Job applicants

CVs, portfolios, cover letters and interview notes provided by people applying to work with us.

## 3. How and why we use it

### 3.1 Lawful bases

- To deliver services and manage our contract with clients: performance of a contract.
- To send invoices and keep financial records: legal obligation.
- To improve our website and understand how it is used: legitimate interests.
- To send occasional updates about our work to client contacts: legitimate interests, and you can opt out at any time.
- To assess job applications: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract.

### 3.2 No automated decisions

We do not make decisions about individuals based solely on automated processing.

## 4. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as we need it:

- Invoices and financial records: seven years after the end of the financial year they relate to, to meet tax obligations.
- Client correspondence and project files: two years after the end of the last engagement with that client, after which they are deleted or anonymised.
- Website analytics: 14 months.
- Unsuccessful job applications: six months after the role is filled, unless you ask us to keep your details for future roles.

## 5. Where data is stored

Our primary systems, including email, file storage and our project management tool, store data in data centres in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area. Where a supplier transfers data outside these areas, we rely on the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or an adequacy decision.

## 6. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with suppliers who help us run the business, such as our email and file storage providers, accounting software, payment provider and analytics provider, and with professional advisers such as our accountants. A current list of these subprocessors is available on request. We do not sell personal data.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to access your personal data, correct it, ask us to delete it, restrict or object to our processing, and ask for a copy in a portable format. To exercise these rights, contact our data protection lead. We respond within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

## 8. Cookies

Our website uses only cookies that are strictly necessary to run it, plus one analytics cookie that expires after 14 months, which is set only if you accept it in the cookie banner. Client applications we build for others follow those clients' own cookie policies.

## 9. Security

We use multi-factor authentication on all business systems, encrypt laptops, and limit access to client systems to the people working on that client. Suspected personal data breaches are reported to affected clients without undue delay and, in any case, within 48 hours of discovery, as required by the Data Processing Addendum.

## 10. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice. Significant changes will be emailed to current client contacts at least 14 days before they take effect.
